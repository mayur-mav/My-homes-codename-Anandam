My Home Codename Anandam is a landmark 52-acre integrated biophilic forest township by My Home Group on the Kadugodi-Hoskote Road (SH-35), in the high-growth Whitefield Annex corridor of East Bengaluru: 2, 2.5, 3 and 4 BHK high-rise residences across iconic G+38 to G+48 towers, planned around an 80% open forest sanctuary.

Conceived as a tranquil nature sanctuary away from urban congestion, four-fifths of the 52-acre township stays green (80% dedicated open landscape), featuring over 3,000 native shade trees, continuous pedestrian eco-trails, resort-style swimming pools, sensory pocket gardens, outdoor sports arenas, and vehicle-free podiums.

These are smartly engineered luxury apartments from 1,225 sq ft to 2,650 sq ft, designed for modern tech professionals and discerning families who seek a premium, well-connected home close to Kadugodi Metro and Whitefield's tech corridor, backed by My Home Group's 40-year legacy of engineering precision. Perfect for families seeking a premium long-term lifestyle and smart investors targeting early-stage appreciation backed by My Home Group.

— THE PROJECT —

My Home Codename Anandam Whitefield Annex,
Project Overview

A landmark 52-acre integrated biophilic forest township along the Kadugodi-Hoskote Road (SH-35). Engineered by South India's premier Grade-A builder My Home Group, offering G+38 to G+48 high-rise residential towers, an iconic 1,00,000+ sq. ft. central clubhouse campus, and 80% open-to-sky greenery with over 3,000 native trees.

A 52-acre biophilic forest sanctuary: 80% of the 52-acre township is dedicated open landscape, featuring 3,000+ native trees, continuous pedestrian forest trails, and vehicle-free podium levels.
Sky-scraping residential towers (G+38 to G+48): Modern architectural towers rising up to G+48 floors, engineered for low density with 4 to 6 units per floor, offering uninterrupted 360-degree skyline views across Whitefield.
Four versatile luxury typologies: from a 1,225 sq ft 2 BHK Luxury to an expansive 2,650 sq ft 4 BHK Presidential suite, each residence featuring expansive viewing decks, private foyers, and three-side ventilation.
A mega clubhouse of 1,00,000+ sq ft: multi-level clubhouse on a dedicated 2-acre campus featuring olympic-length swimming pools, indoor badminton/squash courts, fitness spa, and banquet halls.
The high-growth corridor of Whitefield: located directly on Kadugodi-Hoskote Road (SH-35), 10 mins to Kadugodi Purple Line Metro, and fast access to ITPL and Old Madras Road (NH-75).
